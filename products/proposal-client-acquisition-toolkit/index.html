Overview

A clearer path from enquiry to signed project

A good proposal is not just a price at the end of a call. It shows that you understood the problem, defines what is included, makes the next step obvious, and gives the client confidence that the project has a plan.

This toolkit covers the full conversation around that proposal: the questions you ask, the scope you agree, the price you present, the follow-up you send, and the handoff you complete when the work is done.

What this helps you do

  • Turn discovery notes into a structured proposal without starting from a blank page
  • Separate what is included from what is not, before work begins
  • Price a service consistently instead of improvising on every call
  • Follow up after sending a proposal without sounding pushy or disappearing

Who it is for

  • Freelancers who regularly send proposals
  • Small service businesses that need a repeatable sales process
  • Consultants, designers, developers, and creative studios

Questions

Proposal Toolkit questions

Are the proposal templates editable?

Yes. The proposal, scope, questionnaire, email, and closing documents are editable DOCX files. Adapt the wording, sections, and placeholders to match your services and tone.

Does this include contracts?

No. It includes proposal, scope, discovery, outreach, follow-up, and closing documents. Have a qualified professional review any contract or legal terms you use with clients.

Can I use the templates for different services?

Yes. The structure works for design, development, consulting, marketing, writing, and other service projects. Adjust the deliverables, assumptions, and pricing sections to fit each engagement.

What is included in the toolkit?
