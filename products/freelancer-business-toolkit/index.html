Overview

A practical back office for solo client work

Freelance work often breaks down in the gaps between doing the work and running the business: the quote that never gets followed up, the project details spread across email, or the invoice that is sent late because the tracker is out of date.

This toolkit gives those tasks a simple home. You get editable documents for the client-facing moments and spreadsheets for the records you need to keep. Nothing requires a new platform, a subscription, or a complicated setup.

What this helps you do

  • Create a professional invoice and quotation without rebuilding the layout each time
  • Use one onboarding checklist so every client gets the same clear start
  • See project status, invoice status, and payment follow-ups in one place
  • Keep a straightforward record of income and expenses for your own review

Who it is for

  • Solo freelancers and independent consultants
  • Designers, developers, writers, marketers, and other service providers
  • Freelancers who handle their own admin and client communication

Questions

Freelancer Toolkit questions

Are the templates editable?

Yes. The Word documents are editable in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, and other tools that support DOCX files. The spreadsheets are editable in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, and compatible spreadsheet apps.

Do I need accounting software?

No. The trackers are intentionally simple so you can use them on their own. If you already use accounting software, you can still use the client documents and project tracker alongside it.

Can I use the templates for client work?

You can use the documents and spreadsheets in your own freelance business and adapt them for your client engagements. Do not resell or redistribute the templates as templates.

What is included in the toolkit?

The toolkit is delivered as one ZIP pack through Lemon Squeezy after purchase. The pack includes the PDF usage guide, five Word documents, and two Excel workbooks.
