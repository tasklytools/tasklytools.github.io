Overview

Know the numbers behind your business

You do not need a complex finance stack to understand a small business. You need a consistent way to record income and costs, a profit and loss view you can read, and a few models that answer practical questions before you make a decision.

This toolkit keeps the structure clear and the inputs visible. Use it to track the month, test a price change, estimate a break-even point, and build a short review routine you can repeat.

What this helps you do

  • Record revenue and expenses without mixing them into one unstructured list
  • See gross profit, operating costs, and net profit in a monthly format
  • Test a price, cost, or volume change before applying it to the business
  • Use a monthly checklist so the numbers get reviewed instead of ignored

Who it is for

  • Small business owners who manage their own numbers
  • Solo operators and early-stage service businesses
  • Freelancers who want a clearer financial routine as they grow

Questions

Finance Toolkit questions

Do the spreadsheets include formulas?

Yes. The trackers, profit and loss statement, pricing calculator, and break-even model use formulas and sample rows so you can see how the inputs connect. Clear the samples before adding your own data.

Can I use Google Sheets?

The workbooks are XLSX files and can be imported into Google Sheets. Most formulas and formatting carry over, but check the data validation and calculated columns after importing.

Is this a replacement for an accountant?

No. It is a practical record-keeping and planning toolkit, not accounting, tax, or financial advice. Use your accountant or adviser for decisions that depend on your local rules and circumstances.
