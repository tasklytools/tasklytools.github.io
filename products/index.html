Questions

Toolkit and checkout questions

What is included in a TasklyTools toolkit?

Each toolkit includes a mix of editable DOCX templates, XLSX spreadsheets, and a PDF usage guide. Every product page lists what is included, and the complete ZIP pack is delivered securely after purchase.

Are the files editable?

Yes. Word documents are editable in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, and compatible apps. Spreadsheets are editable in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, and compatible apps. Check formulas and formatting after importing into another app.

How does checkout work?

TasklyTools uses Lemon Squeezy hosted checkout. The buy buttons show whether checkout is available, and no payment is taken while it is not connected.

Can I use the templates commercially?

You can use and adapt the files in your own business and client work. Do not resell or redistribute the templates as templates. Review the licence note on each product page.

How will files be delivered?

Lemon Squeezy handles payment, receipts, and secure digital delivery. Paid files are not available for public download.
