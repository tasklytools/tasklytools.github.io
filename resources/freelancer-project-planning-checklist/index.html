What is inside

A simple plan you can use before the work starts

The checklist is designed for a single freelance project. Use it at kickoff, keep it open during delivery, and review it before the final invoice.

Brief and kickoff

  • Client, project, decision-maker, and main contact
  • Problem, desired outcome, and success measure
  • Constraints, assumptions, and client dependencies

Scope and approvals

  • Deliverables, exclusions, and review rounds
  • Price, deposit, payment schedule, and change process
  • Written approval before work begins

Delivery and schedule

  • Milestones, dates, and who owns each next step
  • Communication rhythm and feedback deadlines
  • Access, content, and approval dependencies

Invoice and closeout

  • Final delivery check and handoff notes
  • Invoice reference, amount, and payment terms
  • Project review, files archive, and follow-up date

How to use it

Keep the checklist close to the work

It is most useful when it becomes part of the project routine, not a document you fill in once and forget.

  1. Before the kickoff: fill in the brief, contacts, outcome, and known constraints.
  2. At scope approval: confirm deliverables, exclusions, price, and payment schedule.
  3. During delivery: update milestones, decisions, and client dependencies as they change.
  4. Before the final invoice: run the delivery, handoff, and closeout checks.

Adapt it to the project. A small retainer needs fewer fields than a multi-stage website build. Remove what does not help and add the one detail you keep forgetting.
