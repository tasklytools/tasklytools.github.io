How it works

Build a rate from the bottom up

Start with the money you need, then divide it by the hours you can actually bill.

  1. Enter the annual income you want to take home after tax.
  2. Add an estimated tax rate and your annual business expenses.
  3. Choose a profit buffer for slow periods, reinvestment, and negotiation room.
  4. Set realistic working weeks, weekly hours, and billable utilization. Non-billable admin and sales time still counts as work.
  5. Use the hourly rate as your anchor, then compare the daily, weekly, monthly, and project rates.

The formulas used

  • Pre-tax incomeTake-home / (1 - tax rate)
  • Target revenuePre-tax income + expenses + buffer
  • Billable hoursWeeks x hours x utilization
  • Hourly rateTarget revenue / billable hours
  • Project rateHourly rate x project hours

Daily and weekly rates use your billable hours; the monthly rate is the annual target divided by 12. Your actual schedule may differ.

FAQ

Freelance Rate Calculator questions

Short answers about assumptions, utilization, tax, and privacy.

How is the freelance hourly rate calculated?

The calculator first works out the pre-tax income needed to reach your take-home goal, adds annual business expenses and your profit buffer, then divides the target revenue by your billable hours for the year.

What is billable utilization?

Billable utilization is the share of your working hours that you can invoice. Admin, sales, learning, and communication time are usually not billable, so a utilization below 100% produces a higher hourly rate.

Should I include taxes in my freelance rate?

Yes. Enter an estimated tax rate so the calculator can work out the pre-tax income needed to reach your take-home goal. The result is an estimate, not tax advice.

Why is the calculated rate higher than a salary equivalent?

A freelance rate has to cover taxes, business expenses, non-billable time, and a buffer for slow periods. It is not the same as dividing a salary by 2,080 hours.

How is the project rate calculated?

The project rate multiplies your hourly rate by the typical number of hours for a project. Adjust the final quote for scope, risk, and the value of the outcome.

Is my financial data stored?

No. The calculator runs locally in your browser. Your income goal, expenses, and assumptions are not sent to a TasklyTools server.
