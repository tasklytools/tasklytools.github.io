FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

What the tools are, how they handle your data, and what is coming next.

Are TasklyTools free to use?

The core calculators and generators are free and run in your browser. The site includes three digital toolkits prepared as paid products. Lemon Squeezy checkout is not connected yet, so no payment can be taken on this site.

Do the tools send my data to a server?

The free calculators and generators process information in your browser. Invoice, proposal, profit, rate, and UTM details are not sent to a TasklyTools server. If a future feature needs an external service, it will be disclosed on that tool page.

Do I need an account to use the tools?

No. You can open a free tool and use it without creating an account. A future Pro tier may offer optional saved presets, which could require sign-in.

What is TasklyTools Pro?

TasklyTools Pro is a planned premium tier for templates, saved presets, and additional exports. Billing is not active yet, and no subscription can be purchased today.

Can I use the generated links and outputs commercially?

Yes. Outputs generated by the free tools can be used in your own work. Review the terms of any third-party platform you use them with, especially advertising and analytics platforms.

Are the digital toolkits available now?

The three toolkit pages and previews are available to review now, but the paid files are not publicly downloadable. Lemon Squeezy checkout is not connected yet, so no payment can be taken. The planned Pro tier is a separate future product.
